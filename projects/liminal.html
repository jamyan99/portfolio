Year

Overview

Many people with anxiety and depression get stuck in inaction—trapped in cycles of overthinking and emotional paralysis. What they don't realize is that this stuckness spreads, shaping their environment into one that mirrors their inner hopelessness. It's not just an internal struggle—it becomes a shared atmosphere of disconnection and heaviness. Liminal is a wellness app designed to help users gently reprogram their subconscious mind and shift inner thought patterns through personalized, immersive tool.

My Roles

Problem Space

Many young adults crave genuine connection, yet anxiety and depression make reaching out feel impossible—trapping them in cycles of isolation, where they seek temporary escape in digital distractions, ultimately reinforcing the very isolation they wish to overcome

Solution

Ideation

The Proposal

A digital self-help tool for individuals experiencing anxiety and depression, helping them break free from cycles of overthinking and inaction

Features

SubLab

Definition
is a tool that lets users create their own subliminal audio tracks—designed to reprogram their subconscious mind using personalized affirmations layered under music or ambient sounds
How It Works
Type your affirmations, choose your background sound, and let the Composer automatically lower the volume of your voice just below conscious hearing. Then listen during sleep, work, or meditation

Meditation

Definition
it's a space for mental rewiring. It combines guided prompts, ambient sounds, and intentional silence to help users tap into deeper brain states and reprogram thought patterns from the inside out
How It Works
Choose your style, guided or solo meditation, personalize the vibe, and end your session with depth

Journal

Definition
Journaling isn't just about writing—it's about pattern recognition and self-clarity. It's a tool to observe your internal dialogue, track emotional loops, and reprogram your story over time
How It Works
Choose your entry type, set your intention, unlock deeper layers, and close the loop

Community

Definition
Community is a space where users can connect, share, and support one another through personal affirmations, reflections, and healing thoughts
How It Works
Post affirmations or reflections, engage with others, choose private or anonymous posting, and filter by topic

Final Delivery

Introducing Liminal

Onboarding

The onboarding starts by introducing the home screen and how to add habits that fit your life, but most importantly it highlights SubLab, since many users were confused about creating subliminal messages, and clearly explains how it works

SubLab

Choose your frequencies, enter your affirmations, pick a background sound, and let the Composer lower your voice just below conscious hearing, then listen while you sleep, work, or meditate

Meditation

Choose between solo or guided meditation. If you select solo, you can create your own track, while guided meditation lets you pick from the audio we've prepared for you

Journal

Learn how to build habits, track your mood and performance, and write your daily journal

Profile & Community

Check your achievements and streaks, post your own affirmations or save others', engage with the community, and filter content by topic
